## Source policy

- Learn segmentation, matrix thinking, diversity and uncertainty from these references.
- Never copy a public game's numeric thresholds as a universal standard.
- A PvE roster can use clear rate, content coverage and build concentration instead of PvP win/ban metrics.
- Meta health is defined by the target game's intended decision diversity, not by equal usage.
